    SAFE SHARING

    Posting & privacy rules

    These rules apply to new posts and later edits, including private posts. All posts are Only me during this development phase; Public is not available yet.

    1. No links or contact destinations: Do not include URLs, website domains, link shorteners, invitation codes that form URLs, e-mail addresses, QR-code instructions or disguised links in the story description or title.
    2. Only authorized media: Post photos you created or have permission to use. No copyright theft, impersonation, fraudulent endorsements or deceptive content.
    3. Respect people and children: Get consent when required from identifiable people. Do not expose minors or upload sexualized, exploitative or abusive material. Obtain parent/guardian consent when appropriate.
    4. Protect privacy and safety: Remove addresses, private contact details, school/work schedules, exact live locations, identifying documents and other sensitive information from photos and text.
    5. Respect the community: No threats, hate, bullying, graphic abuse, dangerous or illegal material, phishing, spam, or scams. Content may be restricted or removed after safety review.
    6. Visibility and control: Only me content is protected by owner access rules. You can edit/delete your own posts; future public posts will include Hide/Unhide. Published content, moderation and reporting are NOT enabled today.

    Checkboxes are author statements, not verification. Text link detection is a browser-side safeguard, not a tamper-proof server rule. Full public posting will require enforced backend screening, moderation and reporting.
